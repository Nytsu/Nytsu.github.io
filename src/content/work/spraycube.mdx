<Section id="problem">
Agriculture often wastes nutrients and chemicals, applying more than a plant actually needs. SprayCube was built to sense and dose more precisely, cutting that waste.
</Section>

<Section id="what-i-built">
A plant nutrient sensing and dosing device, built as Team #1 FlashPose for the 2021 Puerto Rico Science Trust "Challenge to Innovate" program. FlashPose was NASA's C-based algorithm for terrain estimation and vehicle positioning, offered as a starting point; since SprayCube needed a different kind of recognition, we built our own computer vision approach instead.

<ProjectModel src="/models/spraycube.glb" alt="A 3D model of the SprayCube device" caption="SprayCube, modeled in SketchUp, May 20, 2021." />
</Section>

<Section id="how-it-works">
A camera on the device captures the plant, and custom computer vision code (built instead of using NASA's original algorithm) judges what the plant needs. The device then doses nutrients and chemicals accordingly, instead of applying a fixed amount every time.
</Section>
