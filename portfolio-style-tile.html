JustIn: wireless fencing scoring

Every touch, counted.

Role

Scope
Hardware, firmware, app, brand
Status

The problem

Traditional fencing scoring needs a cable running the length of the piste. That makes setup slow and leaves athletes without any record of how their bouts actually went.

What I built

A pair of wireless units that talk to each other and to a phone, plus an app that turns every bout into something an athlete or coach can review.

Type

One family, two weights. Hierarchy comes from size and spacing, not color. Body text is 17px with generous line height, and lines stay near 60 characters.
